# 42. FAQ

### What is Bidii?

Bidii is an AI-powered school management platform that connects student records, CBC academics, attendance, finance, library, boarding, communication, discipline, achievements, administration, analytics and school intelligence in one system.

### Is Bidii built for Kenyan schools?

Yes. Bidii is designed around Kenyan school operations and the country's transition toward competency-based education.

### Does Bidii support CBC?

Yes. Bidii supports the Learning Area → Strand → Sub-Strand structure and performance levels including EE, ME, AE and BE.

### Does Bidii support 8-4-4?

Bidii can support multiple Kenyan assessment frameworks, including 8-4-4, CBC and CBE. However, Bidii's academic experience is positioned primarily toward the CBC era.

### What can Bidii AI do?

Bidii AI can assist with timetable generation, school analysis, report remarks, discipline summaries, communication drafting and natural-language questions through Soma AI.

### What is Soma AI?

Soma is Bidii's built-in AI assistant that allows authorized users to ask questions about school information using natural language.

### Can Soma access everything?

No. Soma works within the user's authorized access.

### Can Bidii build timetables?

Yes. Bidii supports timetable configuration and AI-assisted timetable generation based on scheduling preferences and constraints.

### Can Bidii manage school fees?

Yes. Bidii supports invoices, payments, balances, debtor information, collection reporting and supported M-Pesa integration.

### Can Bidii manage boarding schools?

Yes. Bidii supports dormitories, rooms, cubicles, beds, allocation, transfers, inspections and boarding analytics.

### Does Bidii have a library system?

Yes. Bidii supports library inventory, QR-based circulation workflows, loans, returns, fines, reservations and library analytics.

### Does Bidii have discipline management?

Yes. Bidii provides structured discipline records, incident history, actions, follow-up, supporting documents, timelines, permissions and AI-assisted summaries.

### Does Bidii track achievements?

Yes. Schools can record learner achievements, awards, competitions, leadership, sports, clubs and other school-defined accomplishments.

### Can discipline and achievements be connected to a learner?

Yes. Relevant records can form part of the learner's broader student history, giving authorized staff a more complete view of the learner's journey.

### Can we import existing data?

Yes. Bidii supports structured bulk imports for major school datasets.
